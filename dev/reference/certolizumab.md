# Certolizumab

Data frame containing the results of 12 trials comparing 7 treatments
for rheumatoid arthritis, analysed in (Dias et al. 2011) .

## Usage

``` r
certolizumab
```

## Format

A data frame with 24 rows and 5 variables:

- study:

  study name

- trt:

  treatment name

- r:

  number of patients achieving ACR50 at 6 months

- n:

  sample size

- disease_duration:

  mean disease duration in years

## References

Dias S, Sutton AJ, Welton NJ, Ades AE (2011). “NICE DSU Technical
Support Document 3: Heterogeneity: subgroups, meta-regression, bias and
bias-adjustment.” National Institute for Health and Care Excellence.
<https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/nice-dsu>.
